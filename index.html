About Us

Intelligent Information Processing (IIP) Lab.

The Intelligent Information Processing (IIP) Lab. is part of the National Key Laboratory of Space-Ground Integrated Information Technology, at Xidian University. Founded in 2014, it has a young and energetic research team, directed by Professor Nannan Wang , and now has 10 academic staffs. The research direction of the lab is in the frontier areas related to artificial intelligence, such as Credible Artificial Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence Generated Content (AIGC), and Embodied Intelligence.

Members of the team have led or participated in many research projects including the National Natural Science Foundation of China, National Science and Technology Support Program, National Natural Science Foundation of China Major Research Program, etc., and published more than 200 papers on top journals, e.g. IEEE TPAMI, TNNLS, TIP, TMM, TC, IJCV, and top conferences, e.g. ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, IJCAI, AAAI.
