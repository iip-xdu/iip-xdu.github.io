Research

Research Projects

Embodied Intelligence

Research on embodied perception, reasoning, and interaction for intelligent agents operating in physical and simulated environments.

  • Video understanding for embodied decision-making
  • Multimodal large language models for autonomous driving
  • Human-robot interaction and collaboration
  • Vision-language-action models for robotic manipulation

Visual Understanding

Research on image and video understanding, multimodal perception, and trustworthy visual analysis.

  • Image and video understanding and recognition
  • Multimodal large language models and vision-language learning
  • Pedestrian re-identification in social security
  • Explainable and trustworthy visual analysis (XAI)

Artificial Intelligence Generated Content (AIGC)

Research on generative models for visual content creation, translation, and quality assessment.

  • Multi-modal driven video generation and editing
  • Generative adversarial networks, diffusion models, and cross-modal learning
  • Face photo-sketch synthesis and artistic style transfer
  • Generated visual quality assessment (GIQA)
